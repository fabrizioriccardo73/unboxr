Privacy Policy

Last updated: October 10, 2026

Introduction

This Privacy Policy describes how Unboxr ("the App", "we", "us", or "our") handles information when you use our mobile application available on the Google Play Store.

Unboxr is an archive file extractor that allows you to open and extract compressed files (ZIP, RAR, 7Z, TAR, GZ, BZ2, XZ and other formats) directly on your Android device.

We are committed to protecting your privacy. Unboxr does not collect, transmit, or share any personal data or usage information.

Information we do not collect

Unboxr is a fully offline application. We want to be completely transparent:

  • No personal data is collected, stored, or transmitted
  • No analytics or tracking tools are integrated
  • No advertising SDKs or networks are used
  • No crash reporting services are embedded
  • No usage statistics are gathered
  • No account or login is required
  • No internet connection is required or used by the App: it does not request the Android internet permission

Permissions and file access

File access. The App uses the Android Storage Access Framework to let you select files and folders through the system file picker. This gives it scoped access to only the archives and destination folders you explicitly select. It does not request general storage permissions. No file data is ever transmitted outside your device.

Install packages (REQUEST_INSTALL_PACKAGES). Used only when you ask the App to install an XAPK, APKM or split APK package you opened. Android asks for your approval before the App can install anything.

Data storage

Unboxr stores a minimal set of user preferences locally on your device (for example the default extraction folder and display settings). This data:

  • Is stored only on your device using Android's standard SharedPreferences
  • Is never transmitted to any server or third party
  • Is automatically deleted when you uninstall the App

Data sharing

We do not share any data with third parties because we do not collect any data. There is no data to share.

Children's privacy

Unboxr does not collect any personal information from anyone, including children under the age of 13. The App is safe for use by users of all ages.

Security

Since Unboxr operates entirely offline and does not collect or transmit any data, there is no risk of data breaches or unauthorized access to your information through our App. All file operations are performed locally on your device.

Changes to this Privacy Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. We will notify you of any changes by updating the "Last updated" date at the top of this page. You are advised to review this Privacy Policy periodically for any changes.
